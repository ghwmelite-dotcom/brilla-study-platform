## TIPS FOR OUTREACH

### Finding Contact Information

1. **LinkedIn** — Most direct route to Richie Mensah
2. **Lynx Group website** — Check for contact form or team emails
3. **ACN social media** — Often respond to DMs
4. **Mutual connections** — Ask if anyone can make an introduction
5. **Events** — Attend Lynx events, Masterminds podcast recordings

### Best Practices

1. **Be concise** — Busy people skim emails
2. **Lead with value** — What's in it for them?
3. **Show traction** — Real platforms, real users
4. **Have a specific ask** — "20 minutes of your time"
5. **Follow up** — But not more than 2-3 times

### What to Have Ready

Before you reach out, ensure you can:
- Give a 2-minute elevator pitch
- Demo both platforms smoothly
- Answer: "Why should we manage you?"
- State your specific ask clearly
- Discuss commission/partnership terms

### Timing

- **Best days:** Tuesday–Thursday
- **Best time:** 9–11 AM (catch morning inbox check)
- **Avoid:** Monday mornings, Friday afternoons, weekends
