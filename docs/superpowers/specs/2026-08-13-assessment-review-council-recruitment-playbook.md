# BrillaPrep Assessment Review Council

## Team Recruitment and Onboarding Playbook

**Document status:** Approved future operations and implementation playbook

**Implementation status:** Not implemented

**Use:** Post-main-platform pilot only

**Companion specification:** [Assessment Review Council Deferred Implementation Specification](./2026-08-13-assessment-review-council-deferred-implementation.md)

**Prepared:** 13 August 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

This playbook tells the BrillaPrep operations team how to recruit, vet, appoint and onboard the smallest credible Assessment Review Council team. It also defines the recruitment workflow Kimi Code must eventually implement inside the tutor and administrator portals.

The objective is not to assemble a large committee. The objective is to appoint a compact, dependable team that can prove the quality of one subject before BrillaPrep expands.

For the first subject, recruit:

- One Head of Assessment and Standards.
- One Content Quality and Reviewer Operations Manager.
- Two Accredited Reviewers.
- One Subject Adjudicator.
- One pre-qualified reserve expert who receives no ongoing retainer or assignment payment until activated. A disclosed calibration-assessment honorarium may still apply.

Only the two operating roles are permanent. Reviewers, the adjudicator and the reserve are contracted by assignment.

---

## 2. Recruitment Principles

1. **Recruit for the first subject, not for the whole examination system.**
2. **Use invitation and referral sourcing, not an unrestricted public application form.**
3. **Apply hard eligibility gates before interviews.** Do not spend senior time interviewing ineligible candidates.
4. **Test real assessment judgment with synthetic calibration items.** Qualifications alone do not prove review quality.
5. **Separate recruitment evidence from public profile information.**
6. **Pay for actual work and disclose the reward structure before calibration.**
7. **Never charge an application, vetting or onboarding fee.**
8. **Do not use candidate calibration work in the commercial question bank.**
9. **Active WAEC examiners require recorded written WAEC clearance before assignment.**
10. **Do not trade quality for speed.** Efficiency comes from a narrow funnel and clear decisions, not weaker checks.
11. **Do not reward agreement or punish justified dissent.**
12. **Maintain a reserve candidate to avoid restarting recruitment when one expert becomes unavailable.**

---

## 3. Recruitment Ownership

### 3.1 Before appointing the Head of Assessment

The product owner owns recruitment for the Head of Assessment. The Content Quality and Reviewer Operations Manager may be recruited at the same time, but the Head should participate in final selection if timing permits.

### 3.2 After appointing the Head of Assessment

The Head of Assessment owns academic selection for the first subject. The Operations Manager owns administration, evidence verification, scheduling and candidate communication.

### 3.3 Separation of duties

- Operations verifies that evidence exists and checks references.
- The Head scores subject expertise and calibration performance.
- The product owner approves the two permanent appointments and pilot budget.
- A candidate's close relative, current direct report or business partner cannot be their sole evaluator.
- No person may verify their own documents, approve their own appointment or approve their own payment.

---

## 4. Recruitment Order

Recruit in this order:

1. Head of Assessment and Standards.
2. Content Quality and Reviewer Operations Manager.
3. First-subject adjudicator.
4. Reviewer A and Reviewer B.
5. One reserve expert.

Why this order:

- The Head helps construct the calibration set and judges subject expertise.
- Operations prevents the Head from becoming overloaded with scheduling and document administration.
- Selecting the adjudicator before the reviewers provides an independent senior assessor for difficult calibration decisions.
- The reserve protects the pilot from absence, conflicts or poor probation performance.

If one person temporarily combines the two operating roles, the product owner must retain appointment and payout approval. Academic review and financial approval must never collapse into one person.

## 5. Candidate Profiles

### 5.1 Head of Assessment and Standards

Preferred evidence:

- Former chief examiner, team leader, senior examiner, assessment specialist or equivalent assessment leadership.
- At least ten years of relevant teaching or assessment experience.
- Demonstrated moderation, marking coordination or curriculum-alignment work.
- Strong written reasoning and ability to explain standards consistently.
- Experience managing professionals without compromising academic independence.
- No unresolved professional-integrity issue.

This is a BrillaPrep standard, not a claim about WAEC appointment requirements.

### 5.2 Content Quality and Reviewer Operations Manager

Preferred evidence:

- Experience in academic administration, publishing operations, examination administration, quality assurance or controlled content workflows.
- Excellent document verification, scheduling and record-keeping discipline.
- Comfort with secure digital tools, structured data and audit trails.
- Ability to communicate respectfully with senior educators.
- Strong confidentiality and conflict-management judgment.

The Operations Manager does not need to be a former examiner but must understand examination integrity.

### 5.3 Accredited Reviewer

Minimum gate for a WASSCE subject:

- A university degree in or clearly relevant to the subject.
- At least three years of teaching the subject at an appropriate level.
- Independently verifiable professional history.
- Pass the BrillaPrep subject calibration and probation process.

WAEC publishes the degree and three-year teaching requirement for WASSCE examiners. BrillaPrep may set a higher threshold but must not describe its independently vetted reviewers as WAEC-appointed examiners. Reference: [WAEC Ghana FAQ](https://waecgh.org/faq/).

Minimum gate for a BECE subject:

- A recognized teaching qualification appropriate to the subject and level.
- Relevant teaching experience.
- Independently verifiable professional history.
- Pass the BrillaPrep subject calibration and probation process.

WAEC states a Post-Secondary Teachers' Certificate as its minimum BECE examiner qualification. BrillaPrep should prefer stronger evidence where the subject and candidate pool allow it. Reference: [WAEC Ghana FAQ](https://waecgh.org/faq/).

### 5.4 Subject Adjudicator

Preferred evidence:

- Meets all reviewer requirements.
- At least seven years of relevant teaching or assessment experience.
- Former examiner, team leader, curriculum specialist or demonstrated assessment leader preferred.
- Can resolve valid alternative answers without forcing artificial consensus.
- Can document decisions clearly enough for audit and reviewer learning.

### 5.5 Reserve expert

The reserve passes the same eligibility and calibration gates as a primary reviewer. The reserve signs confidentiality and conflict commitments but receives no ongoing retainer or assignment compensation until formally assigned. A previously disclosed calibration-assessment honorarium may still apply.

Do not advertise the reserve as an appointed Council reviewer before an appointment begins.

## 6. Efficient Sourcing Strategy

Use a referral-first sourcing pyramid. Move to broader channels only if a tier does not produce enough qualified candidates.

### Tier 1: Trusted professional referrals

Ask each credible education leader for no more than two nominations. Good referral sources include:

- Retired heads of department and retired school leaders.
- Former examiners and former examination team leaders whose status can be verified.
- Assessment and curriculum specialists.
- High-performing school heads.
- University education and subject-department leaders.
- Existing verified BrillaPrep tutors after the platform pilot.

Request nominations, not endorsements. Every nominee still completes the full process.

### Tier 2: Targeted institutional outreach

Seek permission to circulate a concise expression-of-interest notice through:

- Ghana National Association of Teachers professional-development or member channels.
- Other relevant graduate-teacher and subject associations.
- University of Cape Coast Institute of Education, particularly its assessment and outreach networks.
- University of Education, Winneba subject and education departments.
- Colleges of Education for BECE subject expertise.
- Selected public and private schools with strong subject departments.

Useful official starting points:

- [Ghana National Association of Teachers](https://www.ghanateachers.com/)
- [University of Cape Coast Institute of Education](https://ioe.ucc.edu.gh/)
- [University of Education, Winneba](https://uew.edu.gh/academics)
- [National Teaching Council Teacher Portal](https://tpg.ntc.gov.gh/)

Institutional outreach is a request to reach members or identify candidates. It must not imply that the institution endorses BrillaPrep.

### Tier 3: Controlled professional discovery

If the first two tiers are insufficient:

- Search LinkedIn for retired or former education professionals with relevant public experience.
- Use moderated teacher communities to request private nominations.
- Ask pilot schools to nominate subject leaders.
- Run a targeted expression of interest with a fixed closing date and invitation-only next stage.

Do not request credential documents through public posts, WhatsApp groups or social-media direct messages. Send shortlisted nominees to BrillaPrep's secure invitation and upload workflow.

### Sourcing target for one subject

Aim to identify:

- 15 credible prospects.
- 10 accepted invitations to an introductory call or information pack.
- 6 completed eligibility applications.
- 4 calibration participants.
- 2 primary reviewers, 1 adjudicator and 1 reserve meeting the final standard.

These are planning targets, not quotas. If fewer than four candidates meet the standard, extend recruitment rather than lowering the pass threshold.

## 7. Two-to-Three-Week Recruitment Sprint

The sprint begins only after the role brief, compensation basis, first subject and assessors are approved.

### Days 0–2: Prepare

- Confirm the first subject and examination level.
- Approve role briefs and scorecards.
- Configure invitation expiry and evidence requirements.
- Prepare a synthetic calibration set and answer key.
- Approve compensation ranges and payment timing.
- Prepare confidentiality, IP, conflict and privacy documents.
- Create the prospect list and assign a source owner to each name.

### Days 3–5: Source and invite

- Request nominations from trusted sources.
- Rank prospects against visible evidence.
- Send personalized invitations to the best 10–15 prospects.
- Hold one optional 30-minute group information session.
- Answer questions consistently using a written FAQ.

### Days 6–8: Eligibility screen

- Verify application completeness.
- Check qualifications, teaching history and NTC status where applicable.
- Identify former- or active-examiner claims.
- Reject clear gate failures quickly and respectfully.
- Schedule structured interviews only for eligible candidates.

### Days 9–12: Assess

- Conduct structured interviews.
- Run the synthetic calibration assessment.
- Score independently before assessors discuss candidates.
- Request references from candidates who remain appointable.

### Days 13–15: Verify and decide

- Complete reference and evidence checks.
- Resolve discrepancies directly with the candidate.
- Hold a documented selection meeting.
- Issue conditional appointment, reserve or rejection decisions.

### Days 16–21: Contract and onboard

- Sign contracts and policies.
- Securely capture payee information.
- Complete platform and confidentiality training.
- Assign calibration feedback and supervised probation work.
- Confirm the pilot schedule and service levels.

The process may take longer when institutions or references respond slowly. Do not treat an unverified claim as verified merely to meet the timetable.

## 8. Hard Eligibility Gates

Apply these before weighted scoring:

- Identity matches application and payment identity.
- Required qualification is evidenced.
- Required teaching or assessment experience is evidenced.
- Subject and examination-level fit is plausible.
- Candidate accepts confidentiality, IP and conflict requirements.
- No prohibited current-examiner activity is identified.
- Active WAEC examiner has valid written WAEC clearance before assignment.
- Candidate can reliably use the digital review workspace.
- Candidate can meet the minimum service level.
- No material unresolved evidence discrepancy remains.

A failed hard gate means `not eligible`, `evidence required` or `clearance pending`; it is not offset by a high interview score.

## 9. Weighted Selection Scorecards

Use a 100-point score after hard gates pass. Two assessors score independently before a selection discussion.

### 9.1 Reviewer and adjudicator scorecard

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Subject mastery | 20 | Qualifications, interview and calibration |
| Assessment and marking judgment | 25 | Calibration decisions and alternative-answer reasoning |
| Syllabus and command-word interpretation | 15 | Calibration and interview |
| Evidence quality and written reasoning | 15 | Structured review sample |
| Integrity, confidentiality and conflict judgment | 15 | Scenario interview and references |
| Digital workflow readiness | 5 | Practical portal task |
| Reliability and communication | 5 | Responsiveness and references |

Recommended decision bands:

- `85–100`: strong appointment candidate.
- `75–84`: appointable subject to references and capacity.
- `65–74`: Associate Reviewer only, with a documented development plan; not eligible as the independent reserve.
- `<65`: do not appoint.

No candidate may be appointed if their assessment-and-marking score or integrity score is below 70% of that dimension's available points, regardless of total score.

### 9.2 Head of Assessment scorecard

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Assessment leadership and moderation experience | 25 |
| Academic and editorial judgment | 20 |
| Governance and integrity judgment | 20 |
| Ability to calibrate and develop reviewers | 15 |
| Written decision quality | 10 |
| Operating practicality and communication | 10 |

### 9.3 Operations Manager scorecard

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Controlled workflow and record-management ability | 25 |
| Confidentiality and evidence-handling judgment | 20 |
| Scheduling and service-level management | 15 |
| Clear candidate and reviewer communication | 15 |
| Financial administration and reconciliation discipline | 15 |
| Digital-tool fluency | 10 |

Do not combine the three scorecards into one generic hiring form.

## 10. Structured Interview Guides

### 10.1 Reviewer or adjudicator interview

Target: 40 minutes.

1. **Experience, 5 minutes:** Explain the candidate's subject and assessment experience without asking for confidential examination information.
2. **Alternative-answer scenario, 10 minutes:** A learner gives a valid method absent from the draft scheme. What should happen?
3. **Ambiguous-question scenario, 10 minutes:** Both listed answers may be defensible. How should the item be reviewed and communicated?
4. **Integrity scenario, 10 minutes:** A former colleague sends a recent paper or internal marking note. What actions should the reviewer take?
5. **Availability, 5 minutes:** Confirm realistic turnaround and conflict risks.

Record structured evidence, not personality impressions.

### 10.2 Head of Assessment interview

Target: 60 minutes.

Include:

- Moderating disagreement without manufacturing consensus.
- Designing a calibration standard.
- Handling a post-publication answer defect.
- Suspending a high-performing but unreliable reviewer.
- Explaining Council independence without implying WAEC endorsement.
- Building a one-subject pilot under a constrained budget.

### 10.3 Operations Manager interview

Target: 45 minutes.

Include:

- Discovering mismatched credential information.
- Protecting private documents.
- Reassigning an overdue review without exposing blind identities.
- Preparing a payout statement with separation of duties.
- Responding to an active examiner whose clearance has expired.
- Communicating rejection respectfully and consistently.

## 11. Calibration Assessment

### 11.1 Design

Use synthetic or rights-cleared items that will not be published as candidate work. The exercise should take 60–90 minutes and include 8–10 items covering:

- Incorrect answer.
- Incomplete marking points.
- Valid alternative method.
- Ambiguous wording.
- Misaligned syllabus tag.
- Incorrect mark allocation.
- Missing diagram or source.
- Bias or accessibility issue.
- Rights or provenance problem.
- A sound item that should pass without unnecessary editing.

### 11.2 Scoring

Score:

- Defect detection.
- Correct disposition.
- Strength of evidence.
- Recognition of legitimate alternatives.
- Ability to distinguish critical from cosmetic issues.
- Clear, respectful correction notes.

Do not reward the number of comments. Reward correct, material and well-supported judgments.

### 11.3 Fairness

- Give every candidate for the same role the same instructions and time allowance.
- Provide reasonable accessibility adjustments.
- Mask candidate identity during calibration scoring where practical.
- Use at least two scorers for borderline or adjudicator candidates.
- Do not use an applicant's calibration output in BrillaPrep content.
- If an assessment requires more than 90 minutes or creates usable commercial work, pay a disclosed assessment honorarium.

## 12. Evidence and Reference Verification

### 12.1 Evidence checks

- Compare names across identity, qualifications and professional records.
- Verify teacher-licence status through the official NTC verification channel where applicable: [Teacher Portal Ghana](https://tpg.ntc.gov.gh/).
- Contact issuing institutions when evidence is unclear.
- Obtain candidate consent before contacting employers, associations or WAEC.
- Verify former-examiner claims through appropriate documentary or professional evidence without requesting confidential material.
- Record `verified`, `unverified`, `discrepancy`, `expired` or `not applicable` for each item.

Do not copy more identity information than the Council needs.

### 12.2 Reference questions

Ask each referee the same core questions:

1. In what capacity and for how long have you known the candidate?
2. What subject and level did the candidate teach, assess or supervise?
3. How consistently does the candidate apply standards across different learners or schools?
4. How does the candidate handle disagreement or ambiguous answers?
5. Can the candidate be trusted with confidential academic material?
6. Does the candidate meet deadlines without sacrificing accuracy?
7. Are you aware of any conflict or integrity concern relevant to this work?
8. Would you appoint this person to review high-stakes educational content? Why?

Record the referee's identity, relationship and response date. Do not accept an unverified text message as the only reference evidence.

## 13. Selection Meeting

Participants:

- Product owner or delegate.
- Head of Assessment, unless that role is being selected.
- Operations Manager or an independent note-taker.

Decision sequence:

1. Confirm hard gates.
2. Review independent scores without changing them.
3. Discuss material score differences.
4. Review calibration blockers.
5. Review references and evidence discrepancies.
6. Check conflicts and availability.
7. Decide `appoint`, `conditional appoint`, `associate/probation`, `reserve`, `defer` or `reject`.
8. Record a short evidence-based rationale.

Do not rank candidates publicly. Do not store protected or irrelevant personal observations in decision notes.

---

## 14. Compensation and Offer Design

Disclose before candidates accept calibration:

- Whether calibration is paid.
- Base honorarium basis.
- Complexity weighting.
- Expected turnaround.
- Quality-pool eligibility.
- Payment approval and timing.
- No guaranteed assignment volume for contracted experts.
- Whether reserve status carries any retainer. The recommended first pilot has no reserve retainer.

Do not hard-code rates until the BrillaPrep pilot and Council budget are approved.

Recommended payment principles:

- Pay a qualifying completed review even when it recommends rejection.
- Do not reduce payment for justified disagreement.
- Do not pay for incomplete, plagiarized or procedurally invalid work.
- Give the reviewer a statement showing calculation and status.
- Target payment or reconciliation within 10 business days after approval.
- Capture MoMo or bank details through a secured form, not email or WhatsApp.

Contracts should be reviewed for Ghanaian employment, tax, privacy and intellectual-property implications before use. The intended assignment-based model must not be labelled a contractor relationship if actual working conditions legally create employment.

## 16. Onboarding Programme

### Session 1: Council purpose and boundaries, 45 minutes

- Why BrillaPrep uses independent review.
- Difference between BrillaPrep accreditation and WAEC appointment.
- Prohibited materials and active-examiner clearance.
- Rights, provenance and non-endorsement language.

### Session 2: Review standard, 90 minutes

- Structured review dimensions.
- Correct answers and valid alternatives.
- Marking points and method marks.
- Syllabus and command-word alignment.
- Bias, accessibility and diagram checks.
- Critical versus cosmetic defects.

### Session 3: Platform and security, 60 minutes

- Secure sign-in and account protection.
- Assignment acceptance and fresh conflict declaration.
- Blind review boundaries.
- Draft, submission and lock behavior.
- Document and data handling.
- Reporting suspected confidential content.

### Session 4: Rewards and professional conduct, 30 minutes

- Honorarium qualification.
- Quality-score dimensions.
- Why consensus rate is not rewarded.
- Disputes, corrections and sanctions.

### Completion gate

The reviewer must:

- Pass a short policy knowledge check.
- Complete a practice review in the portal.
- Confirm access and notification settings.
- Sign all appointment documents.
- Enter supervised probation.

## 17. Probation and Promotion

### 17.1 Probation

Default probation is ten supervised reviews.

During probation:

- The Associate's decision does not count as one of the two independent publication votes.
- A Senior Reviewer or Adjudicator scores each review.
- Feedback is delivered in batches to avoid teaching to individual hidden calibration answers.
- Reliability, evidence quality and policy compliance are tracked.
- Payment policy for probation work is disclosed in advance.

### 17.2 Probation review points

- After review 3: early coaching check.
- After review 6: risk and consistency check.
- After review 10: accreditation decision.

Possible decisions:

- Promote to Accredited Reviewer.
- Extend probation with a written development plan.
- Narrow subject or question-type scope.
- Move to reserve.
- End appointment.

### 17.3 Promotion

Promotion depends on a sufficient evidence window, not tenure alone. Consider:

- Hidden calibration accuracy.
- Defect-free publication record.
- Evidence quality.
- Reliability.
- Conflict and confidentiality compliance.
- Mentoring quality for senior levels.

## 18. Capacity and Continuity

For each activated subject:

- Maintain two primary reviewers.
- Maintain one independent adjudicator.
- Maintain one pre-qualified reserve.
- Limit active assignments per reviewer through configuration.
- Record planned unavailability.
- Do not assign urgent work that compromises double review.

Trigger reserve activation when:

- A primary reviewer declines due to conflict.
- An assignment is overdue beyond the service-level grace period.
- A reviewer is suspended or unavailable.
- Review volume exceeds safe capacity.

If no qualified independent pair exists, pause the subject. Do not use the same person twice or let the adjudicator pre-review and then adjudicate.

---

## 19. Candidate Experience Standards

Efficiency includes respecting candidates' time.

- Acknowledge an accepted invitation immediately.
- Review application completeness within two business days.
- Request missing evidence once in a consolidated message.
- Give at least three business days for calibration unless mutually agreed.
- Communicate the decision within three business days after final evidence arrives.
- Explain whether rejection relates to eligibility, evidence, calibration or current capacity.
- Do not disclose comparative candidate scores.
- Allow a candidate to correct a factual evidence error.
- Provide a privacy contact and evidence-retention summary.

No candidate should repeatedly upload the same document because internal reviewers failed to coordinate.

## 20. Recruitment KPIs

Track by role, source and subject:

- Prospects identified.
- Invitation acceptance rate.
- Application completion rate.
- Eligibility pass rate.
- Median evidence-verification time.
- Calibration completion and pass rate.
- Reference response time.
- Time to conditional offer.
- Time to activated appointment.
- Cost per activated reviewer.
- Ninety-day retention.
- Probation completion rate.
- First-20-review defect and timeliness outcomes.
- Source quality: activated reviewers and performance by sourcing channel.
- Representation across gender, region, public/private-school experience and accessibility needs, reported only in privacy-safe aggregates.

Initial operating targets:

- Fill one subject panel within 21 calendar days when evidence and references respond on time.
- Review application completeness within two business days.
- Complete internal evidence checks within three business days of receiving complete evidence.
- Achieve 100% signed policy completion before account activation.
- Achieve 100% valid subject accreditation before assignment.
- Achieve 100% clearance validation for active WAEC examiners.

Do not set a target calibration pass rate. The standard determines the pass rate, not the reverse.

## 21. Stop and Escalation Rules

Pause recruitment or appointment when:

- The content-rights path is unresolved.
- Compensation budget is not approved.
- No independent calibration assessor is available.
- Candidate evidence cannot be stored securely.
- An active-examiner clearance is unclear.
- A referral source demands control over selection.
- The candidate pool is too small to create independent review and adjudication.
- Material credential discrepancies remain unresolved.
- The recruitment timetable pressures the team to reduce a quality gate.

Escalate suspected forged credentials, confidential examination material or conflicts to the Rights and Integrity process. Do not investigate through informal group chats.

## 22. Recruitment Portal Requirements for Kimi Code

### 22.1 Administrator workflow

The portal must allow authorized administrators to:

- Create a prospect record without uploading sensitive documents.
- Record nomination source and consent status.
- Issue an expiring, subject-scoped invitation.
- Track invitation acceptance.
- Review application completeness.
- Request missing evidence in one consolidated action.
- Assign independent scorecard assessors.
- Schedule or release calibration.
- Capture structured interview scores.
- Request and record references.
- Record evidence verification status.
- Hold a selection decision with rationale.
- Issue conditional appointment, reserve or rejection.
- Generate an onboarding checklist.
- Activate capabilities only after all gates pass.
- Export privacy-safe recruitment metrics.

### 22.2 Candidate workflow

The invited candidate must be able to:

- Verify the invitation and authenticated email.
- View role, subject, compensation basis and privacy notice before applying.
- Save an application draft.
- Upload evidence securely.
- Declare former- or active-examiner status.
- Consent to verification.
- Declare conflicts.
- Choose interview availability.
- Complete calibration within an accessible workspace.
- View application stage and outstanding actions.
- Sign appointment documents.
- Complete onboarding and policy checks.

### 22.3 Required recruitment states

`prospect` → `invited` → `invitation_accepted` → `application_in_progress` → `eligibility_review` → `assessment` → `references` → `selection` → (`conditional_offer`, `reserve`, `deferred`, `rejected`) → `contracting` → `onboarding` → `probation` → `accredited`

Exceptional states:

- `withdrawn`
- `invitation_expired`
- `evidence_required`
- `clearance_pending`
- `suspended`
- `appointment_ended`

The server controls transitions. Administrators cannot jump directly from `invited` to `accredited`.

### 22.4 Additional data requirements

In addition to the companion specification's tables, implementation may require:

- `reviewer_prospects`
- `reviewer_assessments`
- `reviewer_scorecards`
- `reviewer_references`
- `reviewer_onboarding_tasks`
- `reviewer_appointment_documents`

Do not create these blindly. First compare them with the then-current schema and determine which data belongs in normalized tables versus immutable, versioned JSON snapshots.

### 22.5 Automation boundaries

The system may automate:

- Invitation expiry.
- Completeness checks.
- Reminder scheduling.
- Score aggregation.
- Gate validation.
- Onboarding checklist progression.
- Metric calculation.

The system must not automate:

- Credential-verification decisions.
- Final academic suitability decisions.
- Integrity-risk dismissal without human review.
- Active-WAEC-examiner clearance interpretation.
- Appointment approval.

## 23. Operational Templates

### 23.1 Nomination request

**Subject:** Private nomination request: BrillaPrep subject assessment specialist

> BrillaPrep is preparing a future, independently governed review panel for [EXAMINATION] [SUBJECT]. We are seeking experienced educators with strong assessment judgment and integrity. Please nominate no more than two people whom we may privately invite to learn about the role. A nomination is not an endorsement or appointment, and every nominee will complete the same independent vetting and calibration process. Please do not send certificates or confidential examination material in response to this message.

### 23.2 Candidate invitation

**Subject:** Invitation to apply: BrillaPrep Assessment Review Council

> You have been nominated for consideration as a [ROLE] in [EXAMINATION] [SUBJECT]. The Council is independent and does not represent WAEC. Participation is assignment-based and subject to identity, qualification, experience, conflict, calibration and reference checks. Active WAEC examiners cannot participate without written WAEC clearance. Before deciding, you will be shown the scope, compensation basis, privacy notice and expected time commitment. Your secure invitation expires on [DATE].

### 23.3 Evidence request

> Your application is complete except for the items listed below. Please use the secure upload link; do not email or send credentials through WhatsApp. Requested items: [CONSOLIDATED LIST]. We will use them only for the stated verification purpose and retain them according to the Council privacy policy.

### 23.4 Reference request

> [CANDIDATE] has authorized BrillaPrep to contact you regarding a subject-review role. The role concerns educational-content quality and requires confidentiality, consistent assessment judgment and dependable delivery. Please use the secure reference form or request a scheduled call. Do not share candidate scripts or confidential examination information.

### 23.5 Conditional appointment

> We are pleased to offer you a conditional appointment as [ROLE] for [EXAMINATION] [SUBJECT]. This is not a WAEC appointment or endorsement. Activation depends on completion of the listed agreements, onboarding, conflict declaration and probation requirements. The appointment is assignment-based, and no minimum volume is guaranteed unless expressly stated in the attached terms.

### 23.6 Reserve decision

> You met the current standard, but the active panel has been filled. With your consent, BrillaPrep would like to retain your verified status as a reserve candidate until [DATE]. Reserve status does not create an assignment or payment entitlement. We will request fresh availability and conflict confirmation before any activation.

### 23.7 Respectful rejection

> Thank you for completing the BrillaPrep review process. We will not proceed with an appointment at this time. The decision relates to [eligibility / evidence / calibration / current capacity]. It does not affect your general tutor account. You may request correction of any factual record used in the decision, but comparative candidate scores and confidential assessor notes cannot be provided.

## 25. Definition of Recruitment Done

Recruitment for the first subject is complete only when:

1. Both permanent operating appointments are active, or an approved temporary separation-of-duties arrangement exists.
2. Two independent reviewers are accredited for the exact examination and subject.
3. One independent adjudicator is accredited for the exact examination and subject.
4. One reserve candidate has passed the required gates.
5. Every active person has signed the appointment pack.
