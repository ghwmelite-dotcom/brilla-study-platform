## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Creating Your Account

**Step 1: Visit the Website**
- Go to [brillaprep.org](https://brillaprep.org)
- Click "Get Started Free" or "Sign Up"

**Step 2: Choose Your Role**
- **Student**: For JHS and SHS students
- **Teacher**: For educators (requires approval)
- **Parent**: To monitor your child's progress

**Step 3: Fill in Your Details**
- Full name
- Email address
- Password (at least 8 characters)
- School name
- School level (JHS or SHS)
- Year/Form

**Step 4: Complete Registration**
- Click "Create Account"
- You'll be logged in automatically
- Start exploring!

### 1.2 Logging In

1. Go to brillaprep.org
2. Click "Login"
3. Enter your email and password
4. Click "Sign In"

**Forgot Password?**
1. Click "Forgot Password"
2. Enter your email
3. Check your inbox for reset link
4. Create a new password

### 1.3 Your Profile

Access your profile by clicking your avatar (top-right):

- **Edit Profile**: Update name, school, profile picture
- **Settings**: Notification preferences, theme
- **Logout**: Sign out of your account

## 3. Practice Modes

### 3.1 Topic Drill

Perfect for focused learning on specific topics.

**How to Use:**
1. Go to **Practice** from the sidebar
2. Select **Topic Drill**
3. Choose your subject (Math, Physics, etc.)
4. Select a topic
5. Set difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard)
6. Choose number of questions
7. Click **Start Practice**

**During Practice:**
- Read each question carefully
- Select your answer
- Get immediate feedback
- Click "Explain" for AI help
- Track your progress at the top

### 3.2 Speed Race

Train for quick recall - NSMQ style!

**How to Use:**
1. Go to **Practice**
2. Select **Speed Race**
3. Choose subject
4. Click **Start Race**

**Rules:**
- 10 seconds per question
- Answer as fast as you can
- Correct answers earn more points
- Wrong answers = no points
- Try to beat your high score!

### 3.3 Flashcard Mode

Study using spaced repetition.

**How to Use:**
1. Go to **Practice**
2. Select **Flashcards**
3. Choose a deck or create your own
4. Click **Start Studying**

**During Study:**
1. Read the front of the card
2. Try to recall the answer
3. Click to reveal the back
4. Rate your confidence (1-5)
5. Algorithm schedules next review

### 3.4 Mock Exams

Full exam simulation for the real experience.

**How to Use:**
1. Go to **Mock Exams**
2. Choose exam type (WASSCE, BECE, NSMQ)
3. Select subject and paper
4. Click **Start Exam**

**Features:**
- Realistic timing
- All question types
- No pausing allowed
- Comprehensive results
- Performance breakdown

## 4. AI Tutor

Your personal AI assistant for learning help.

### 4.1 Opening the AI Tutor

- Click the **sparkles icon** (bottom-right corner)
- Or click "Ask AI" on any question

### 4.2 What You Can Ask

- **Explain concepts**: "Explain photosynthesis"
- **Solve problems**: "How do I solve this equation?"
- **Get study tips**: "How should I prepare for WASSCE?"
- **Clarify doubts**: "I don't understand Newton's 3rd law"

### 4.3 Uploading Images (NEW!)

**Perfect for homework help!**

1. Click the **paperclip icon** (📎)
2. Select your image (photo of homework, diagram, etc.)
3. Add a message like "Help me solve this"
4. Click Send

**What AI Can Analyze:**
- Handwritten math problems
- Science diagrams
- Chemistry equations
- Physics circuits
- Past paper questions
- Textbook pages

**Limits:**
- Max 3 files per message
- Max 10MB per file
- Supported: JPG, PNG, GIF, PDF

### 4.4 Getting Hints

Instead of the full answer, ask for hints:

1. During a question, click "Get Hint"
2. Level 1: Gentle nudge
3. Level 2: More specific
4. Level 3: Strong hint

### 4.5 Step-by-Step Solutions

For complex problems:

1. Click "Step-by-Step"
2. AI breaks down the solution
3. Explains each step
4. Shows the method to use

## 5. Flashcards

### 5.1 Creating a Deck

1. Go to **Practice** → **Flashcards**
2. Click **Create Deck**
3. Enter deck name
4. Select subject (optional)
5. Click **Create**

### 5.2 Adding Cards

1. Open your deck
2. Click **Add Card**
3. Enter the front (question)
4. Enter the back (answer)
5. Add image if needed
6. Click **Save**

### 5.3 Studying Cards

1. Open a deck
2. Click **Study Now**
3. View the front, try to remember
4. Click to flip and see answer
5. Rate your confidence:
   - **1**: Didn't know it
   - **2**: Hard to remember
   - **3**: Got it with effort
   - **4**: Easy!
   - **5**: Too easy

### 5.4 Spaced Repetition

The system automatically:
- Shows difficult cards more often
- Shows easy cards less frequently
- Optimizes your learning time

### 5.5 Public Decks

Browse decks created by teachers:

1. Go to **Flashcards**
2. Click **Browse Public Decks**
3. Filter by subject
4. Click to preview
5. **Save to My Decks** to use

## 7. Competitions & Battles

### 7.1 NSMQ Mode

Experience all 5 rounds:

**Round 1: Fundamentals**
- Basic recall questions
- 30 seconds each
- Individual scoring

**Round 2: Speed Race**
- First correct answer wins
- 10 seconds max
- Quick thinking required!

**Round 3: Problem of the Day**
- One complex problem
- 5 minutes to solve
- Partial credit available

**Round 4: True or False**
- Rapid-fire questions
- +2 for correct, -2 for wrong
- Be careful!

**Round 5: Riddles**
- Word puzzles
- 3 clues given
- More points for fewer clues used

### 7.2 1v1 Battles

Challenge others to real-time battles:

**Starting a Battle:**
1. Go to **Battles**
2. Click **Find Opponent**
3. Wait for matchmaking
4. Battle begins!

**During Battle:**
- Same questions, same time
- Answer faster for bonus points
- See opponent's progress
- First to finish wins ties

**Rewards:**
- Win: +100 XP
- Participation: +25 XP

### 7.3 Leaderboards

Check your ranking:

1. Go to **Leaderboard**
2. View by:
   - **National**: All users
   - **School**: Your school
   - **Weekly**: This week's top performers
   - **Subject**: Per subject ranking

## 11. Tips for Success

### 11.1 Daily Habits

1. **Practice every day** - Even 15 minutes helps!
2. **Complete daily quests** - Maximize your XP
3. **Maintain your streak** - Consistency is key
4. **Review mistakes** - Learn from errors

### 11.2 Effective Study

1. **Start with weaknesses** - Improve where you need it most
2. **Use flashcards** - Great for memorization
3. **Take mock exams** - Simulate real conditions
4. **Ask the AI Tutor** - Don't stay confused

### 11.3 Competition Mode

1. **Practice speed race daily** - Improve quick recall
2. **Try all NSMQ rounds** - Get comfortable with each format
3. **Challenge friends** - Battles make learning fun
4. **Review explanations** - Understand, don't just memorize

### 11.4 Before Exams

1. **Take full mock exams** - Build stamina
2. **Review past papers** - Know the format
3. **Focus on weak areas** - Use analytics to guide you
4. **Get enough sleep** - Rest is important!

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions

### Account & Access

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on login page, enter your email, check inbox for reset link.

**Q: Can I use Brilla on multiple devices?**
A: Yes! Your account syncs across all devices.

**Q: Is Brilla free?**
A: Basic features are free. Premium features require subscription.

### Practice & Learning

**Q: How is XP calculated?**
A: 10 XP per correct answer, bonuses for streaks, quests, and battles.

**Q: What happens if I break my streak?**
A: Streak resets to 0, but you keep all XP earned.

**Q: Can I redo questions I got wrong?**
A: Yes! Wrong questions appear more in future practice.

### AI Features

**Q: Is the AI Tutor always available?**
A: Yes, 24/7. If issues occur, try refreshing.

**Q: Can I upload any image?**
A: Only educational images (homework, diagrams). Max 10MB.

**Q: How accurate is AI essay grading?**
A: Very accurate for structure and grammar. Teacher review available.

### Technical

**Q: The app is slow, what do I do?**
A: Try clearing cache, check internet, or reinstall.

**Q: I can't log in, what's wrong?**
A: Check email/password, ensure account is approved (teachers).

**Q: How do I contact support?**
A: Email support@brillaprep.org or use in-app Help Center.

### 13.2 Creating Classes

6. View results when complete

1. Go to **Library**
2. Click **Upload Resource**

4. Add metadata (title, subject, etc.)
