# Presentation Tips

## Before the Meeting

1. **Research Recent Lynx News**
   - Check for any new announcements
   - Reference recent Richie interviews/podcasts

2. **Prepare Live Demos**
   - BrillaPrep: Show quiz functionality, dashboard
   - TradeMetricsPro: Show calculator, backtest results

3. **Know Your Numbers**
   - Current users/revenue
   - Growth metrics
   - Market size stats

## During the Presentation

1. **Connect to Richie's Philosophy**
   - Quote "build systems that outlive talent" early
   - Show you understand his approach

2. **Be Specific About Mutual Benefit**
   - Don't just ask for help
   - Show what they gain

3. **Have a Clear Ask**
   - Not vague "let's work together"
   - Specific: "Strategic partnership with pilot project"

4. **Show Traction**
   - Real numbers, real users
   - Not just ideas — working platforms

## Follow Up

1. Send thank you email within 24 hours
2. Include one-page summary
3. Offer specific next step (pilot project proposal)
4. Set timeline for decision
